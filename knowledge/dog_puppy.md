# Puppy care (dogs under 1 year)

- Feeding: 3 small meals a day at regular times (for example morning, midday, evening). About 10 minutes each.
- Potty breaks: take the puppy outside after every meal and after naps. Short trips of about 5 minutes, several times a day.
- Walks: keep walks short, about 5 minutes per month of age, up to twice a day. Avoid long or intense exercise while joints develop.
- Training: 2 to 3 short sessions of 5 to 10 minutes a day (sit, name recognition, crate). Short sessions work better than long ones.
- Socialization: a few minutes a day of calm exposure to new people, sounds, and surfaces.
- Teeth and grooming: gentle brushing and handling of paws and ears for about 5 minutes, a few times a week, so the puppy gets used to it.
