# Senior cat care (11 years and older)

- Feeding: 2 to 3 smaller meals a day; seniors often eat less per meal. About 5 minutes each.
- Medications: give any prescribed medications (for example thyroid or kidney meds) at the same time every day. High priority.
- Litter box: scoop daily and keep the box easy to step into (about 5 minutes).
- Play: short, gentle play sessions of 5 to 10 minutes a day keep older cats engaged.
- Grooming: brush a few times a week (about 10 minutes); older cats groom themselves less.
- Hydration: refresh water daily; seniors are prone to dehydration.
