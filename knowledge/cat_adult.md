# Adult cat care (1 to 10 years)

- Feeding: 2 meals a day at regular times, about 12 hours apart. About 5 minutes each.
- Litter box: scoop once a day (about 5 minutes) and fully change the litter weekly (about 15 minutes).
- Play: at least 1 to 2 interactive play sessions of 10 to 15 minutes a day to keep the cat active and prevent weight gain.
- Grooming: brush short-haired cats weekly and long-haired cats several times a week (about 10 minutes).
- Fresh water: rinse and refill the water bowl or fountain daily (about 2 minutes).
