# General pet care (all pets)

- Fresh water every day: clean and refill water bowls daily (about 2 minutes).
- Consistent routine: feed and exercise at roughly the same times each day; pets cope better with predictable schedules.
- Medications: if a pet has prescribed medication, give it at the same time daily and treat it as high priority.
- Habitat cleaning: clean the pet's living area (cage, tank, bedding, or litter) on a regular schedule, daily or weekly depending on the animal.
- Daily check: spend a few minutes looking over the pet for changes in appetite, energy, or behavior.
