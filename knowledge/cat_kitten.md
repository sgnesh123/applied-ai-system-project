# Kitten care (cats under 1 year)

- Feeding: 3 to 4 small meals a day for kittens under 6 months, then 2 to 3 meals a day. About 5 minutes each.
- Litter box: scoop at least once a day, ideally twice (about 5 minutes).
- Play: 2 to 3 interactive play sessions of 10 to 15 minutes a day (wand toys, chasing). Kittens have lots of energy.
- Handling: a few minutes a day of gentle handling of paws, ears, and mouth to make later grooming and vet visits easier.
- Scratching: encourage use of a scratching post with short daily sessions of praise or treats.
