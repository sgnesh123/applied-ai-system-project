# Adult dog care (1 to 7 years)

- Feeding: 2 meals a day, about 12 hours apart. About 10 minutes each.
- Walks: at least 2 walks a day. Most adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of total exercise daily; high-energy breeds need more.
- Enrichment: 10 to 20 minutes a day of play, puzzle toys, or sniffing games to prevent boredom.
- Grooming: brush 1 to 3 times a week depending on coat (about 10 to 15 minutes). Brush teeth several times a week.
- Fresh water should always be available; rinse and refill the bowl daily (about 2 minutes).
