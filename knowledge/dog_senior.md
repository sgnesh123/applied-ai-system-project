# Senior dog care (8 years and older)

- Feeding: 2 meals a day at consistent times. Many seniors need a lower-calorie or joint-support diet as advised by a vet.
- Walks: shorter, gentler walks more often (for example 2 to 3 walks of 15 to 20 minutes) instead of one long walk.
- Medications: give any prescribed medications at the same time every day; these are high priority and should not be skipped.
- Mobility: a few minutes of gentle stretching or slow play helps stiff joints.
- Grooming: brush coat and teeth a few times a week (about 10 minutes) and check for new lumps or sore spots while grooming.
